Odoo 18 Technical Service Maintenance Professional Reports

Professional Technical Service Management for Odoo

ADK Service Technique provides a structured workspace for technical interventions, maintenance operations, sites, zones, equipment, anomalies, diagnoses, corrective actions and reporting. It is designed for organizations that need clear field operations and reliable management visibility from one application.

⚠ User Roles & Access Rights — Please Read Before Purchasing

This module includes two access levels. Please review them below so the permissions match your team's needs before you buy.

Manager

Full access: can create, edit and delete sites, blocks, equipment, maintenance types, teams and interventions. Sees the Configuration menu and can print global reports.

Agent / User

Can create and follow up interventions, and manage equipment day-to-day (no deletion). Read-only access to sites, blocks, maintenance types and teams. No access to the Configuration menu.

Key Features

A complete technical service workflow, from field intervention to management reporting.

Professional Dashboard

Monitor interventions, resolution rates, equipment issues, sites and team activity from a centralized dashboard.

Intervention Management

Create and track interventions with anomaly details, diagnosis, measures taken, corrective actions and status.

Sites, Zones and Equipment

Organize technical locations into sites and blocks, then associate equipment with the correct operational location.

Maintenance Teams

Build technical teams from Odoo employees and assign field responsibilities in a structured way.

Anomalies and Corrective Actions

Document detected anomalies, technical diagnoses, actions taken and follow-up information throughout the intervention.

Automatic PDF Reports

Generate structured intervention, equipment and global service reports with practical filtering options.

Role-Based Access

Separate field operations from management with dedicated access levels for technical agents and managers.

How It Works

A clear five-step workflow from technical configuration to professional reporting.

01. Configure sites, blocks and teams

Set up your technical locations and maintenance teams before starting field operations.

02. Register and organize equipment

Keep technical information, location, status, acquisition and warranty details in one place.

03. Create an intervention

Record the maintenance type, location, equipment, control point and assigned team when an issue is identified.

04. Document diagnosis and corrective actions

Follow the intervention, record the diagnosis and measures taken, and document the corrective action through completion.

05. Monitor performance and reporting

Use the dashboard to monitor activity, open issues, resolution rates and service performance.
